Guidelines and phoneme chart

Generally speaking, follow this procedure:

  1. Carefully check if all stress markers are placed correctly.
  2. Check for vowel lowering (check /e/ and /i/ or vice versa, same with /o/ and /u/).
  3. Check for any dropped glottal stops.
